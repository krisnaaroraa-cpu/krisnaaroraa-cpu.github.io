Privacy policy

This policy applies to Personal YouTube Uploader, a personal-use app operated by Krisna Arora. Effective 8 October 2026.

  • Purpose: upload the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channels and check the upload results. Google account access is used only for that purpose.
  • Data handled: video files, thumbnails when supplied, titles, descriptions, visibility settings, channel identifiers, video identifiers and upload status. The app also stores OAuth credentials so it can perform authorized uploads without repeated sign-in.
  • Use and storage: credentials are kept in encrypted credential storage. Video files and upload metadata may be retained as needed for an upload, retries, result checks and the owner's records. Access is limited to the owner and service providers helping operate the personal app.
  • Sharing: video files and metadata are sent to Google/YouTube to perform the requested upload. Hosting, storage and operational service providers may process the data needed to run the app. Data is not sold or used for advertising, and is not shared with unrelated people.
  • Control and deletion: the owner can revoke the app's access in Google Account permissions and request deletion of stored credentials, files or records by contacting the address below. Revoking access does not delete videos already on YouTube; those can be deleted in YouTube Studio.
  • Security and limits: encryption and restricted access are used to protect credentials. No system can guarantee uninterrupted access or perfect security.
  • Changes: this page will be updated if the app's data practices change.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
