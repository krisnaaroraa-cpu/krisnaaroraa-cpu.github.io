Personal Video Uploader

A personal tool for uploading to the owner's own YouTube channels.

A personal tool

Personal Video Uploader is Krisna Arora's private-use app for uploading videos to his own YouTube channels. It is not a public upload service and does not accept accounts from other people.

With the account owner's permission, it uploads video files and their titles, descriptions and visibility settings, and reads channel and video information to check upload results.

This app uses YouTube API Services. Google account permission is required, and it can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
