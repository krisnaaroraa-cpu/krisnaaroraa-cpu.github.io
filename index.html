Personal YouTube Uploader

A personal tool for uploading to the owner's YouTube channels.

A personal tool

Personal YouTube Uploader is Krisna Arora's private-use app for uploading videos to his own YouTube channels. It is not a public upload service and does not accept accounts from other people.

With the account owner's permission, it uploads video files and their titles, descriptions and visibility settings, and reads channel and video information to check upload results.

Google account permission is required. Permission can be revoked at any time. Google may still require a security check.
